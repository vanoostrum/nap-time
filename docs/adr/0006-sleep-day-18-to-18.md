# A sleep day runs from 18:00 to 18:00

A calendar day cuts every night in two, so a child who sleeps from 19:30 to 06:30 would show two half nights. Nap Time counts sleep per sleep day, from 18:00 to 18:00, named after the date it ends on: a night that starts after 18:00 counts for the morning it ends in. The week, the averages, the bars, the timeline, and the day sheet all use sleep days.

A sleep counts whole for the sleep day it starts in, also when it crosses 18:00, so the totals add up to the sleeps a parent sees on that day. Only the timeline, which shows clock time, draws a sleep that crosses 18:00 in both rows. (Theo, 2026-11-25: before this, a sleep was split at 18:00, and a nap from 16:00 to 18:30 counted only 2 h on its day.) A sleep day that holds a running sleep stays "so far" after 18:00 until that sleep ends, so a total never grows on a counted day.

The home screen's "slept today" counts the sleep day named after today's date. Before 18:00 that is the current sleep day. From 18:00 to midnight it is the sleep day that just ended, so a parent still sees the day's total in the evening, before the child goes to bed. (Theo, 2026-11-14)
