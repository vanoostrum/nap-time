# A sleep day runs from 18:00 to 18:00

A calendar day cuts every night in two, so a child who sleeps from 19:30 to 06:30 would show two half nights. Nap Time counts sleep per sleep day, from 18:00 to 18:00, named after the date it ends on: a night counts for the morning it ends in. A sleep that crosses 18:00 is split at 18:00 for the hours per day. The week, the averages, the bars, the timeline, and the day sheet all use sleep days.

The home screen's "slept today" counts the sleep day named after today's date. Before 18:00 that is the current sleep day. From 18:00 to midnight it is the sleep day that just ended, so a parent still sees the day's total in the evening, before the child goes to bed. (Theo, 2026-11-14)
