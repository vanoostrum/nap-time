# The sky uses the sun times of Utrecht, with no location permission

The home screen sky follows the real sunset and sunrise. The family lives in the Netherlands, and Theo chose not to ask for location (2026-11-14). So Core computes the sun times for Utrecht (52.0907° N, 5.1214° E), in the phone's time zone, for every date. A reader may expect the phone's own location: this is deliberate.

Sunset and sunrise each last from 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after the sun time. A running sleep, night or nap, follows the sun too, so the sky turns to sunrise while the night timer still runs (Theo, 2026-11-23). Until then, a running night showed the night sky at any time. Test mode has no location argument, because the app has no location. (2026-11-15)
